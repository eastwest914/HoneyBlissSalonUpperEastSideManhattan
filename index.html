FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about balayage hair color, our services and our Upper East Side salon.

What is balayage hair color?

Balayage is a freehand hair painting technique used to create natural, sun-kissed highlights that blend seamlessly into your base color. Unlike traditional foil highlights, balayage produces soft, graduated color with a beautiful natural grow-out — perfect for the busy New York City lifestyle where low-maintenance style is essential.

How long does a balayage appointment take?

A full balayage service at Honey Bliss Salon typically takes between 2 and 4 hours depending on your hair length, thickness and the complexity of the look. We recommend booking a consultation so we can give you an accurate time estimate and discuss the best approach for your hair goals.

What is the difference between balayage and ombre?

Balayage is the technique — a freehand painting method used to apply color. Ombre is the style — a gradient from dark roots to lighter ends. Ombre balayage combines both: using the balayage technique to achieve a gradient ombre effect with soft, natural-looking blending rather than harsh lines. We offer both at our Manhattan salon.

How often do I need to touch up my balayage?

One of the greatest advantages of balayage is its low maintenance grow-out. Most clients return every 3 to 6 months for a refresh, compared to every 4 to 8 weeks for traditional highlights. The soft, blended roots mean you never have a harsh line of regrowth — your hair always looks intentional and beautiful.

Do you offer color correction services?

Yes, color correction is one of our specialties at Honey Bliss Salon. Whether your hair has become brassy, too light, too dark, uneven or was damaged by a previous service, our specialists will create a personalized correction plan. We prioritize the health and integrity of your hair while achieving the color result you want. Consultation is recommended for all correction services.
